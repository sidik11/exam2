Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Exam Master (“Exam Master”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you visit or use exammaster.info, including its student, teacher, institute and administrator features.

We aim to collect only information needed to operate accounts, deliver exams, protect platform security and provide results. Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is specifically required for a feature.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account and contact information: name, email address, phone number, login identifiers and account role.
  • Education and exam information: institute or class details you provide, exam answers, scores, attempts, rankings, feedback and progress.
  • Messages and support: information included in complaints, support requests or communications with us.
  • Technical and security information: IP/network details, browser or device information, timestamps, logs and session/security data generated when you use the service.
  • Administrative information: settings and records entered by authorised teachers, institute administrators or platform administrators.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts; authenticate users; deliver exams and show results; maintain rankings and learning progress; provide institute and administration features; respond to support requests; detect fraud, abuse and security incidents; troubleshoot, maintain and improve the platform; and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Legal grounds and choices

Where applicable law requires consent or another lawful basis, we will rely on the relevant basis for the purpose concerned. You can contact us to ask about your information, request correction or deletion where applicable, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Some information may need to be retained to provide the service, preserve exam records, protect security or comply with law.

4. Cookies, sessions and storage

The platform may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to maintain sign-in sessions, remember preferences, support security and keep the service functioning. Blocking these technologies may prevent some features from working correctly.

5. Service providers and sharing

We may process or share relevant information with infrastructure, database, hosting, email-delivery, security and other service providers that help operate Exam Master. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the platform, or as part of a legitimate business reorganisation. We do not intend to sell personal information as a standalone product.

6. Payments

If a payment feature is offered, payment details may be processed by the payment provider shown during checkout. Do not send card numbers, UPI PINs, passwords or one-time passwords to Exam Master support. The provider’s own privacy terms may also apply.

7. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including account operation, exam records, dispute handling, security and legal compliance. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no online service or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children and student accounts

Students should provide accurate information and use the platform with any parent, guardian, school or institute supervision required by applicable law. A parent or authorised guardian may contact us about a child’s information. We will take appropriate steps where required by law.

9. Your requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity and may retain certain records where permitted or required by law. We aim to respond to grievances within 90 days, or sooner where applicable law requires.

10. Third-party links and services

The platform may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are controlled by those providers, so please review their policies separately.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform, our providers or applicable laws change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

12. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact infoexammaster@gmail.com.

This page is a general statement of our current practices and is not legal advice. The operator should review it against the platform’s actual data flows and applicable law.
